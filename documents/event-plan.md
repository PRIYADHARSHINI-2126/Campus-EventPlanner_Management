## 1. Executive Summary & Event Concept
"Udhayam" (meaning "Sunrise" or "Dawn" in Tamil) is KIT Coimbatore's hallmark inter-collegiate techno-cultural celebration. Designed to bridge academic engineering curricula with practical industry problem-solving and cultural dynamism, the 2027 edition brings together undergraduate and postgraduate students from over 45 institutions across South India.

The two-day fest encompasses 14 competitive events organized across specialized academic departments (Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, Information Technology, Mechanical, Aeronautical, Electrical & Electronics, and Biomedical Engineering). Day 1 is dedicated to cutting-edge research paper symposiums, coding challenges, and hardware prototype expos; Day 2 transitions into robotics, creative media arts, quizzes, and a grand cultural valedictory night.

4. **Student Leadership & Volunteer Training:**

## 10. Risk Management, Safety, and Accessibility
* **Electrical Interruption:** Dual 62.5 kVA automatic diesel generators tested 48 hours prior; dedicated electrical maintenance engineer on standby throughout.
* **Overcrowding & Lab Congestion:** Pre-registration capped per event; overflow auditorium balcony opened if attendance exceeds 1,200.
* **Medical Emergency:** First-aid desk at Central Foyer staffed with emergency kits and saline; tie-up with local multi-specialty hospital in Kannampalayam.
* **Campus Accessibility:** Wheelchair ramps at Auditorium entrances and ground floor lab blocks; priority seating for delegates with accessibility requirements.
* **Code of Conduct:** Zero-tolerance anti-ragging policy strictly enforced by the physical education faculty and discipline squad.

## 11. Post-Event Review & Quality Audit
* **Attendance Reconciliation:** Verified registration count versus gate turnstiles.
* **Budget Audit:** Comparison of itemized expenditures against actual bills submitted to the Accounts Office.
* **Satisfaction Survey:** Digital feedback collected before certificate issuance covering 5 core dimensions: Technical Content, Venue Facilities, Registration Ease, Food Quality, and Timeliness.
* **Continuous Improvement Log:** Formal debriefing meeting held within 5 days of event closure to document lessons learned for the subsequent edition.
