Risk Mitigation & Backup Arrangements

Power Failure / Grid Outage High Severity

Automatic 62.5 kVA diesel generator failover configured in 45 seconds; central audio and servers backed by uninterrupted UPS.

Attendance Surge (>1,200 Delegates) Medium Severity

Online pre-registration cutoffs strictly enforced; overflow 300-seat balcony opened with LAN video relay.

Sudden Rain / Inclement Weather Low Severity

All flagship tracks hosted inside concrete auditoriums; outdoor exhibits relocated to covered ground porticos.

Medical Emergency / Dehydration High Severity

First-aid desk in foyer staffed with registered nurse; college emergency van on standby for Kannampalayam hospital transit.

1. Event Objectives & Expected Outcomes

UDHAYAM 2027 is organized to bridge theoretical classroom engineering with practical problem-solving. Core outcomes include: (a) evaluating 120+ research paper abstracts across computing, electronics, and mechanical engineering; (b) displaying 40+ hardware prototypes addressing sustainable agriculture and automotive tech; (c) exposing students to keynote speakers from Coimbatore's industrial hub; and (d) training 80 student committee leaders under Student General Secretary Ms. Priyadharshini.

2. 6-Week Milestone Preparation Schedule

• T-6 Weeks (Jan 10–17): Proposal submission; Principal & Management administrative sanction; auditorium booking.

• T-4 Weeks (Jan 18–31): Chief guest invitation dispatch; printing 150 posters; dispatch to 50 colleges; portal launch.

• T-2 Weeks (Feb 01–14): Research paper screening; catering contract for 1,200 delegates; delegate kit procurement.

• T-1 Week (Feb 15–22): AV sound test; 62.5 kVA diesel generator failover dry-run; local police & medical station tie-up.

• T-1 Day (Feb 25): Stage backdrop flex mounting; pre-assembling 1,200 kits; volunteer walkie-talkie briefing.

• Event Days (Feb 26–27): Live execution; 14 parallel tracks; score audit; grand valedictory awards ceremony.

• T+1 Week (Feb 28–Mar 06): Accounts reconciliation with bills; participant survey audit; digital certificate dispatch.

4. Safety, Risk Management & Accessibility

Backup power is guaranteed via dedicated automated 62.5 kVA diesel generator on standby. In case of rain, all events are securely housed in concrete auditoriums and lab complexes. Emergency first-aid is staffed in the central foyer with nursing assistance and on-call ambulance coverage. Wheelchair ramps provide universal accessibility to the auditorium and laboratory blocks.
